The OPL3 instrument banks of ADLplug-Next
=========================================

resources/opl3/banks.pak holds the 75 instrument banks that the OPL3 plugin of
ADLplug-Next offers, as ADLplug's did. This file gathers what is known of the
origin of each bank and of the terms under which it may be used. It is not a
license. Most of these banks were not made to be shared: they are the
instrument sets of DOS games and sound drivers, taken from those programs, and
for many of them no license or permission is known.

Where the banks come from
-------------------------

ADLplug made the pack with tools/pak-banks.sh from the banks built into
libADLMIDI (https://github.com/Wohlstand/libADLMIDI), and last changed it in
its commit 931c036 of 2019-02-22, which also updated libADLMIDI to commit
c5f8da70da78110dbbaa74ff4ebce6edf5d04be8. ADLplug-Next ships the pack as it
was.

libADLMIDI has kept the numbers of its banks, and records what it knows of
them:

  - fm_banks/list-of-banks.txt describes the banks and where they come from;
  - fm_banks_new/LICENSE-*.txt hold the terms that some of the authors gave;
  - fm_banks_new/greyzone/ holds the banks whose authors gave no permission.

For each bank of the pack, the list at the end gives the libADLMIDI bank of the
same number: its name and file, the note of fm_banks/list-of-banks.txt on it,
and the terms of the next section that the note or the file refers to. All of
these come from the libADLMIDI that ADLplug-Next builds with
(thirdparty/libADLMIDI, commit 99c5e03a5cf9b4fdf35b53efddb853c96320d50f).
libADLMIDI has extracted some of the banks again since 2019, so its files can
differ from the banks in the pack. Where a bank mixes instruments of several
origins, the terms apply only to the instruments they cover; "none recorded"
means that libADLMIDI records no license or permission for the bank.

      Banks designed for Miles Sound System / IBM Audio Interfaces Library.

  libADLMIDI, on the banks of the Apogee Sound System
  (fm_banks/list-of-banks.txt):

      Banks in the format for Apogee Sound System. The library itself has been
      released under GPLv2 in a set of Rise of the Triades source code game release

      (Original IBK files included inside "TIMBRES.ZIP" file)

GREYZONE: banks without the permission of their authors

  libADLMIDI keeps the bank in fm_banks_new/greyzone/, whose README.txt says:

      Here are banks based on the content picked-up without explicit permission of
      their authors. These banks awaiting for their clarification of the legal status.

      Their usage terms are unclear. From the one side, nobody cares about these banks
      and the copyright status is vague once it comes to the sets of 11-byte numbers
      combo. From the another side, the legal status must be clarified for these banks
      so nobody have questions especially from the side of the Debian project where
      copyright question is taken seriously.

      Once any author of each bank will explicitly grant the suitable license, they
      will be moved to the general directory.
      ------------
      And so, until these banks will be fully clarified, there are temporary
      replacements provided in the "placeholders" directory that will take places of
      these banks until they will be fully clarified.
